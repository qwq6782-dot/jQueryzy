Code Trivia

Where did the term algorithm come from?

Algorithm is a mistranslation of the author of a book on mathematics, Muḥammad ibn Mūsā al-Khwārizmī, a Persian mathematician and scientist who lived around 825 CE.

When was the term 'computer' first introduced?

The term computer was first referenced as early as 1613 and meant "one who computes": a person performing mathematical calculations.

When was the oldest known analog computer created?

One of the world's oldest analog computers, the Antikythera mechanism (150 BC, found in Greece) was used to calculate astronomical positions.

What is the difference between analog and digital devices?

An Analog signal is a continuous signal, analogous to another time varying signal. A digital signal uses discrete (discontinuous) values.
